Qwen-CUA is a native computer-use agent built on a 397B-A17B Qwen mixture-of-experts backbone. It observes screenshots and acts through keyboard and mouse events without relying on DOM trees, accessibility metadata, or task-specific APIs.

The technical report describes a cloud rollout fleet with access to nearly 100,000 vCPUs, approximately 40,000 verifiable tasks, and complete-trajectory optimization with verifiable rewards. I contributed to the work as a core contributor.
